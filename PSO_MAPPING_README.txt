CO–PSO MAPPING QUICK GUIDE

1. Sign in to the existing Flask application.
2. Open the dashboard and select a subject.
3. Click View Subject Report. CO–PSO mapping and justifications are shown in the subject report, not on the dashboard.
4. PSOs are maintained manually in Dataset/PSO_Dataset.xlsx. Do not upload PSO data through the website.
5. Keep the columns: PSO ID | Programme Specific Outcomes.
6. Edit the PSO rows in Excel and save. The subject report regenerates/loads mapping from the CO dataset and PSO descriptions.
7. Subject report exports include CO–PSO mapping and justifications.
8. Mapping levels are relative rank suggestions (3 high, 2 moderate, 1 low, 0 outside top three); faculty should validate them.
